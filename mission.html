Our outcome vision

Where we are trying to get to

EA Amsterdam is a well-known breeding ground where anyone driven to make an effective positive impact can find community, connections, knowledge, and the infrastructure to turn good intentions into real change.

In plainer terms

If you arrive wanting to do something useful with your life and you have no idea where to start, we want this to be the place that gets you unstuck — through the people you meet here as much as anything we run.

And if you already know what you are doing, we want to be worth your time too.

Theory of change

How we think we make a difference

Being busy is not the same as having an effect. This is the chain we believe connects what we run to something actually changing in the world.

  1. Introduce people to EA concepts

    Through events, the Introductory Program and ordinary conversation — presented honestly, objections included.

  2. Inspire a more altruistic focus

    Meeting people who take these questions seriously tends to do more than any argument on its own.

  3. Give people the means to act

    Tools, resources, education and connections for those who already want to do good and need a route into it.

  4. Check that it worked

    We survey the community: did contact with EAA cause significant action? If the honest answer is no, the earlier steps need to change.

Holding ourselves to it

What we measure

Attendance is easy to count and mostly beside the point. These are the things we actually watch.

Significant actions taken

People who report that contact with EAA caused a real change — a career step, a project, a pledge. Measured by survey.

Program completions

People finishing the Introductory Program, and how many stay involved afterwards.

Retention

Whether people who come once come back. A community nobody returns to is not doing its job.

Community health

Whether people feel able to disagree, admit confusion and ask for help here.

We would rather know than look good. Where a number is disappointing we would prefer to publish it and change what we do. See our impact for what we have measured so far.
