Amsterdam

Doing good better, together.

Effective altruism is about using evidence and reason to work out how to benefit others as much as possible — and then acting on it.

We are the Amsterdam community built around that idea: people who want their work, donations and time to count for as much as they can. We meet to think hard about the world's most pressing problems, to disagree well, and to help each other turn good intentions into real impact.

New to EA? Join our intro program

Every semester we run the Introductory EA Fellowship: a practical course in doing good more effectively. Each week you read, try an exercise, and then work through it together with a facilitator.

It teaches skills, not conclusions — reasoning transparently, comparing interventions, thinking under uncertainty, prioritising between problems. You finish with a plan for your own impact. No prior knowledge needed, and you are not expected to agree with any of it.
