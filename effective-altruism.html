Effective altruism

Using evidence and reason to work out how to benefit others as much as possible, and taking action on that basis.

Where to read more

The people who explain this best are not us. Everything below is written by organisations who work on it full time.

effectivealtruism.org

The general introduction — what the idea is, where it came from, and what people do with it.

80,000 Hours

Free research and advice on choosing a career that does a lot of good. The most useful thing here if you are deciding what to do with your working life.

GiveWell

Years of in-depth research into which charities do the most good per euro, with all the workings shown.

Giving What We Can

Effective giving, and the pledge to give a percentage of your income to the organisations that use it best.

EA Forum

Where the actual arguments happen, including the sharpest criticism of effective altruism. Worth reading before you decide what you think.

Effective Altruism Netherlands

The national organisation. They run the fellowship we host, and events across the country.

Would rather talk it through than read? That is most of what we do. Our introductory fellowship works through these ideas with a facilitator over six weeks, and our events are open to anyone curious.

A community of students and professionals in Amsterdam working out how to do the most good — and then doing it.
