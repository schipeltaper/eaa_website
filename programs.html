The Introductory EA Program

Six weeks, one session a week, in a small group with a facilitator. Each week has a short set of readings — usually under an hour — and then you spend an hour and a half talking them through with the same handful of people.

It is the single best way into effective altruism. You get the ideas in a sensible order, the strongest objections alongside them, and people to argue with rather than a reading list you will never finish.

Roughly what the six weeks cover

The curriculum gets updated each round, so treat this as the shape rather than the exact syllabus.

  1. The effectiveness mindset

    Why the gap between good and great giving is so large, and what follows from that.

  2. Differences in impact

    Scale, neglectedness and tractability as a way of comparing very different problems.

  3. Radical empathy

    Whose interests count — across distance, species and time — and why that is harder than it sounds.

  4. Our final century?

    Existential risk: pandemics, AI, nuclear weapons, and what taking low-probability catastrophes seriously implies.

  5. What do you think?

    The strongest criticisms of effective altruism, and where you personally land.

  6. What next?

    Turning any of it into a concrete plan — career, giving, projects — and deciding what you will actually do.

Questions people ask

Do I need to know anything about EA beforehand?

No. The program is built for people starting from zero.

Do I have to agree with effective altruism?

No, and it is a better group when not everyone does. Week five is largely about the criticisms. Plenty of people finish unconvinced and stay involved anyway.

What if I miss a week?

It happens — tell your facilitator. Missing more than a couple makes it hard for the group, so if you know it will be a bad six weeks, take the next cohort instead.

Is it only for students?

No. We run groups for students and for working people, usually at different times of day.

When does the next one start?

Each semester. The dates go on the events calendar and in the WhatsApp group as soon as they are set.
