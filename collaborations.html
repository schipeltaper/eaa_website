In the Netherlands

EA Netherlands

The national organisation. They run national retreats and events, support local groups like ours, and host the community health function we share. We follow their code of conduct.

Want to work with us?

We are open to co-hosting events, giving a talk to your association, running a workshop, or helping a group in another city get started. If you have something in mind, tell us what it is and we will tell you honestly whether we can do it well.
