Our place in the ecosystem

Where we fit, and who pays for it

EA Netherlands sits above us

EA Netherlands is the national organisation for the Dutch EA community. They support and mentor us in our community-building efforts — advice, shared materials, national events, and a route to help when something in the community goes wrong.

How we are funded

Our funding comes from the Centre for Effective Altruism and from donations. All of it goes on the expenses of running this thing: snacks, booking rooms, the website, social media ads, and so on.

Nobody in our team receives any compensation. We are all volunteers.

More detail, and how to contribute, on the donate page.

Other EA groups across the Netherlands

We are one of a number of EA groups around the country, at universities and in cities, and we keep in regular contact with them. If you are not in Amsterdam — or you are moving — there is very likely a group near you.

Wider network

Organisations whose research and programs we lean on constantly.

Centre for Effective Altruism

Supports local groups like ours, runs the EA Global conferences, and maintains the curricula our programs are built on.

80,000 Hours

Career research we point people towards constantly, plus free one-to-one advising several of our members have used.

EAGx conferences

Regional conferences, including editions held in Amsterdam. A good first conference once you have been to a few of our events.
