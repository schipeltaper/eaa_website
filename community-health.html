Community health

The code of conduct we follow, and who to talk to if something is wrong.

We want this to be a place where you can think out loud, be wrong, and disagree without it costing you anything. That does not happen by accident, so it is worth being explicit.

Our code of conduct

We follow the code of conduct of EA Netherlands, which sets out how people in the Dutch EA network are expected to treat each other.

Read the code of conduct

If something goes wrong

If you have been made uncomfortable, or seen something that worries you, please tell us. You do not need proof, and you do not need to be the person it happened to.

  • Speak to any organiser, at an event or by message.
  • Or email info@eaamsterdam.com.
  • You can also go outside EAA entirely. EA Netherlands can help in exactly this situation: anyone in the Dutch EA network can reach out to them for a confidential conversation, with no obligation to take it any further.

If you are struggling with something bigger than us, your GP is the usual route in the Netherlands, and 113 Zelfmoordpreventie is free and available around the clock on 0800-0113.
