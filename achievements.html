Being straight about it

How we count

Impact numbers are easy to inflate without meaning to, so it is worth writing down what each of these does and does not claim.

Giving What We Can pledges
People who told us they took a pledge after getting involved here. We cannot see who pledges, so this depends on people telling us — it is a floor, not a total.
Fellowship graduates
People who finished the six-week Introductory EA Fellowship. Finishing, not signing up.
People reached at events
Distinct individuals who came to at least one event, deduplicated across the whole period. Someone at twenty events counts once.
What none of this measures
Whether any of it changed what people actually do. That is the thing we care about, and it needs asking people, not counting them — see what we measure.

Did we cause it? Someone who was already heading in this direction and happened to meet us is not our impact. Where we can, we ask people directly whether contact with EAA made a difference, and we would rather under-claim than over-claim.
