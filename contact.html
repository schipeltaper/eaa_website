Contact

Questions, ideas, or you just want to be told when the next thing is happening — all of it is welcome.

✉️

Email

info@eaamsterdam.com reaches the organising team. Best for anything that needs a real answer — and for asking to be added to the WhatsApp group.

Where events get announced and most of the day-to-day chat happens. Email us and we will send you the invite.

Who to contact about what

I want to come to something
No need to ask — pick anything on the events calendar and turn up. Email us first if you would like someone to look out for you.
I want to join the Introductory Program
See programs, or email us and we will tell you when the next cohort opens.
I would like to speak at an event, or co-host one
Email us with what you have in mind and roughly when.
I represent an organisation and want to collaborate
See collaborations, then email us.
I want to help run EAA
Genuinely welcome — say what you would enjoy doing and how much time you have.
Something happened at an event that I want to raise
Please see community health, which sets out the options including confidential ones outside EAA.
Press or media
Email us and we will get back to you.

Where you will find us

Most student activities happen around UvA Roeterseiland and the VU campus; professional events move around the city. The exact location is always on the event listing.

We are volunteers, so replies usually take a couple of days. If something is time-sensitive, say so in the subject line.
