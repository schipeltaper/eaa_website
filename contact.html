Join us

The WhatsApp community

This is where events get announced, where people ask questions between events, and where plans get made. It is the single best way to stay in the loop, and you are welcome in it whether or not you have been to anything yet.

It is a friendly group, not a busy one — expect a handful of messages a week, mostly about what is coming up. Introduce yourself when you arrive if you like; plenty of people just read for a while first, and that is fine too.

New members are approved by an organiser, so it may be a few hours before you are let in. If the button does not work, or you would rather not use WhatsApp at all, email info@eaamsterdam.com and we will sort it out.

Our one rule for the group: it is for the community, not for advertising. Events, questions, articles and plans are all welcome. Anything that reads like a sales pitch is not.

Tell us what you think

Feedback

If you have feedback on anything — an event, a program, this website, or how the community feels to be part of — we would be very grateful to receive it. Praise is nice; the critical stuff is more useful.

You do not need to soften it, and you do not need to have a solution. "This did not work for me and here is why" is exactly the kind of thing we want to hear.

Send us feedback

If you would rather not put your name to it, say so in the email and we will keep it to ourselves — or raise it through community health, which sets out the confidential routes outside EAA.

Where you will find us

Most student activities happen around UvA Roeterseiland and the VU campus; professional events move around the city. The exact location is always on the event listing.

We are volunteers, so replies usually take a couple of days. If something is time-sensitive, say so in the subject line.

A community of students and professionals in Amsterdam working out how to do the most good — and then doing it.
