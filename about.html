About

Effective altruism asks a simple question: with the resources we have — time, money, skills — how can we do the most good?

Core ideas

  • Impartiality. Everyone's wellbeing counts equally, wherever or whenever they live.
  • Scale, neglectedness, tractability. Some problems are far larger and less crowded than others.
  • Evidence and reason. Good intentions are not enough; results matter and should be measured.
  • Scope for revision. Conclusions are held provisionally and updated as evidence changes.

Common focus areas

  • Global health and development
  • Animal welfare
  • Risks to humanity's long-term future
